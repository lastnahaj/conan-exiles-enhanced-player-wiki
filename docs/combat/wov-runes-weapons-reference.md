# WoV Runes, Tools & Weapons — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
This is the complete ordinary-player rune, tool, and weapon reference from the older VAM guide. It
is preserved locally, but its exact effects and values are not asserted for Enhanced. Use the
current [WoV Arsenals and Armor](./wov-arsenals-armor.md) page for confirmed family names and the
live item panel for current statistics.
:::

## Rune workflow

The Legacy guide documented three rune potencies: **Crude**, **Enhanced**, and **Supreme**. Crude
runes could drop from ordinary enemies and were used to make the stronger versions. Greater runes
and tier-three bow kits required Iris at the Rune Forge.

1. Keep the weapon or tool in the character inventory; an item on the hotbar did not appear in the
   old Rune Assembler.
2. Open the WoV menu and choose the Rune Assembler.
3. Add a slot when necessary; the old guide listed a cost of 50 Mythical Crystals.
4. Apply compatible runes up to the item's available slots. Mixed effects were supported.
5. Remove a rune for base-game gold when needed. Every extraction slightly reduced the item's base
   statistics in the old system.

Runes modified base values, not already multiplied values. The same Harvesting Rune therefore added
the same fixed amount to a basic crystal tool and an Elder tool even though the Elder tool started
higher. Weapon and tool rune modification applied only to VAM items in the older guide.

## Crystal and Elder tools

The guide advised obtaining a WoV pick or pickaxe early because mines, meteors, bosses, and many WoV
materials required a WoV harvesting tool. Rocknoses could still be harvested with an ordinary tool
for a small amount of starter raw gems.

Seven Mythical tool families followed the crystal sequence **White, Yellow, Green, Blue, Red, Pink,
Purple**. Elder tools used five random-stat tiers and required Shadow Twin Andrea at the Elder bench.

| Elder tool tier | Legacy minimum harvest | Legacy maximum harvest |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 15 | 30 |
| 2 | 30 | 60 |
| 3 | 45 | 90 |
| 4 | 60 | 120 |
| 5 | 75 | 150 |

Those ranges applied to the Pick, Hatchet, Sickle, Skinning Knife, Cleaver, and Pickaxe in the old
table. Elder tools were repaired at the Rune Repair Anvil and could take up to five runes.

## Fifteen Legacy arsenals

The old arsenals offered bows, arrows, claws, daggers, great-axes, hammers, maces, spears, swords,
two-handed swords, war-axes, dual variants, and three shield sizes. Damage-over-time effects stacked
and persisted for ten seconds after the last hit. The guide stated that runes did not increase DOT
damage and that most effects did not apply to undead or bosses. These are historical mechanics.

## Special and reforged weapons

- The guide recorded staves and scepters as cosmetic-only work in progress: they were craftable at
  a Sorcery Bench but had unfinished statistics and no rune support.
- Carmen the Plague Mistress could drop the **Grim Reaper Scythe**, a runable weapon whose Legacy DOT
  stacked to 50 and did not expire. Antidotes, Violet Curative, or death removed it in that build.
- Reforged arsenals used a matching hidden knowledge scroll. The old guide said scrolls dropped
  randomly from Valkyries or were purchased with Death Tokens, while reforged bars came from
  dismantling normal VAM weapons.

## Follower warning from the old guide

The guide warned that followers could make runed melee weapons disappear. It recommended giving
followers only unruned melee weapons or bows, including upgraded bows. This behavior is not
independently confirmed for Enhanced; test with disposable equipment before relying on it.
