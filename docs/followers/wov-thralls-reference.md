# WoV Thralls & Workers — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
These locations, names, drops, worker restrictions, and routes come from the older VAM guide. They
are stored locally for reference but do not establish current Enhanced spawns. Internal spawn names
and administrator-only fields from the source are excluded.
:::

## Valkyrie camp structure

The Legacy guide listed five ordinary camp themes—Classic near New Asagarth, Mountain, Desert,
Jungle, and Lava or Volcano—and three special camps: Fallen, Plague, and Blood. It placed portals to
the three special camps in the room opposite the Frost Forge inside the Temple of Frost.

Only **Lesser Valkyries** fit an ordinary Wheel of Pain in that guide. Other tameable Valkyries
required the Rune Prison, which the old progression placed at level 240.

For the supplied level-0 server captures, see [Valkyrie Stats by Location](./wov-valkyrie-stats.md).

## Training Regimen scrolls

The source documented attribute-named scrolls, but said the chosen name did not change the result.
Put a scroll in the follower's inventory to consume it. A normal scroll granted one level and a
Mighty scroll granted two.

The guide strongly warned against using a scroll to cross a perk level: stop at 9, 19, 29, and
similar thresholds, then earn the perk-awarding level through combat. It also listed the base-game
Elixir of Rebirth for rerolling earned perks. Verify all of this against Enhanced before spending a
scroll.
