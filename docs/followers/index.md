# Followers

Three dedicated Enhanced mods improve follower handling:

- [Better Thralls](./better-thralls.md) — group commands and optional follower-management systems.
- [Extended Thrall Stats](./extended-thrall-stats.md) — more information in follower stat panels.
- [Follower Collision](./follower-collision.md) — movement through a player's own followers.

Wings Of Valhalla adds extended follower leveling and two documented breeding loops. See the local
[WoV companions guide](./wov-companions.md) for current Rocknose and Dragon loops, the detailed
[Creatures and Breeding reference](./wov-creatures-reference.md), and the local
[Thralls and Workers reference](./wov-thralls-reference.md).
