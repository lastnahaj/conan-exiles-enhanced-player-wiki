# WoV Companions and Breeding

## Choose the breeding loop

| Companion | Published Enhanced scope | Main input sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rocknose | 24 breedable variants | Supported egg or Pebblestone → breeding station → supported metal or stone |
| Dragon | 14 breedable variants | Supported egg → displayed heating recipe → hatchling → breeding station → meat or fish |

Use the current station preview to evaluate an outcome. The older food-probability tables are
kept in the Legacy archive and do not establish current Enhanced odds.

## Rocknoses

![Official Enhanced Workshop image showing WoV Rocknose variants](/media/mods/wov-rocknoses.png)

The current Enhanced build documents **24 breedable Rocknoses**.

1. Obtain a supported Rocknose egg or Pebblestone.
2. Place the young Rocknose in the breeding station shown by the current recipe.
3. Feed Pebblestone a supported metal or stone.
4. Collect the resulting Rocknose when breeding finishes.

The older VAM guide contains color-specific foods and probabilities. The current Enhanced source
instead describes the broad **metal or stone** feed rule, so the result preview in the breeding
station should be used for the current outcome.

## Dragons

![Official Enhanced Workshop image showing WoV dragon variants](/media/mods/wov-dragons.png)

The current Enhanced build documents **14 breedable Dragons**.

1. Obtain a supported Dragon egg.
2. Heat the egg using the recipe displayed by the current crafting interface.
3. Let the heated egg hatch.
4. Place the hatchling in its breeding station.
5. Feed it meat or fish and collect the resulting Dragon when breeding finishes.

The Legacy-era guide lists color probabilities for individual meat and fish types. Those tables are
Legacy-era references; use the Enhanced station preview for the current result.

## Follower leveling

WoV extends follower progression to level 100 and awards an additional perk every ten levels in its
published system. The follower's current stat panel shows its active level and perks.

The detailed older food tables, probabilities, companion routes, Valkyrie camps, workers, and
training-scroll behavior are stored locally in [Creatures and Breeding](./wov-creatures-reference.md)
and [Thralls and Workers](./wov-thralls-reference.md).
