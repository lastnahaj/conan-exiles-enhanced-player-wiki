# WoV Creatures & Breeding — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
The current Enhanced release confirms 24 breedable Rocknoses and 14 breedable Dragons. The detailed
foods, probabilities, locations, and routes below come from the older VAM guide and are not current
Enhanced proof. Use the [current breeding guide](./wov-companions.md) and live station preview when
the two differ.
:::

## Rocknose loop in the old guide

For crystal Rocknoses, the recorded route was to buy a base Rocknose Egg from Shawna the Strange in
the Den for ten gold coins, let it hatch—faster in a Compost Heap—then place the Pebblenose in a
Rocknose Farm or Animal Pen with the desired crystal.

Essence Rocknose Eggs came from their matching WoV boss, Iris, or Sarpendon. An essence egg could
produce only its matching family. The guide recorded a 25% boss-egg drop chance. After hatching,
these also used the Rocknose Farm.

and Iris Rocknoses had no production row. A Valkyrian Beastmaster accelerated growing and production
in the old system.

## Dragon loop in the old guide

1. Harvest a wild WoV Dragon with a WoV tool to obtain an egg; the guide preferred a pick.
2. Craft a Heated Egg in the Volcanic Forge after learning Dragon Breeder.
3. Let the egg hatch, with the Compost Heap recorded as the faster option.
4. Put the hatchling in a Dragon Lair and select its meat or fish.

Undead Dragons were described as an Alchemy Bench craft rather than part of this food loop. Water
Dragons required the old Water Dragon Breeder knowledge.

## Other Legacy companion routes

| Companion group | Old player route |
| --- | --- |
| Undead Valkyries | Learn Raise Dead, obtain the Necromancer Amulet from the golden-armored undead captain, and craft the follower at the WoV Alchemy Bench. |
| Sema's Lion | Rare drop from the Sema miniboss in the Blood portal. |
| Valkyrian Pet Cage | Rare drop from any Valkyrie. Placed directly without an Animal Pen and gave a random Jungle Jaguar, Fallen Leopard, Desert Lynx, Lava Cat, or Mountain Sabre. |
| VAM Bears | Learn Tamed Valkyrie Bear and feed a base-game Bear Cub Valkyrian Sacred Blood in an Animal Pen. The old guide recorded four possible WoV bears. |
| Abyssal Steeds | Learn Abyssal Steed Breeder and feed a Sturdy Foal a White, Yellow, Green, Blue, Red, Pink, or Purple WoV crystal. These permanent colored mounts required no saddle in the old guide. |

Administrator-only companion routes from the source are not included.
