# WoV Crafting Catalog — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
This page preserves ordinary-player recipes and workflows from the older VAM guide. Recipe names,
effects, values, knowledge requirements, and acquisition routes are historical unless the current
Enhanced crafting panel confirms them. Administrator-only entries from the source are excluded.
:::

## Alchemy

| Recipe group | Legacy use | Old requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragment of Power | Crafted Fragments of Power from glowing pink dust. | Any profession at Apprentice. |
| VAM Seeds | Turned plants into matching seeds. | Grandmaster Alchemist (58). |
| VAM Reagents | Produced reagents used by WoV recipes. | Grandmaster Alchemist (58). |
| VAM Essences | Combined other boss materials into WoV essences. | Grandmaster Alchemist (58). |

## Enslaving equipment

The old guide grouped the **Rune Prison**, **Runeforged Truncheon**, and colored **Enslaver Bows**
under the VAM Enslavement Tools and Rune Enhanced Enslaving knowledge in the Headhunter profession.
It described the Rune Prison as holding twelve thralls, the truncheon as the strongest WoV knockout
weapon, and concussion runes as its recommended modification. Enslaver Bows could not take runes;
the guide recommended compatible bow upgrades and warned that many Valkyries resisted arrows.

## Plants

- WoV plants came from seed pouches and from harvesting seedlings rather than as random drops from
  ordinary plants.
- Seedlings shared old spawn locations with kappas.
- Once a plant was obtained, the Alchemy Bench could turn it into matching seed.
- A WoV worker at the bench increased the old seed result.
- VAM Farmland doubled plant results in the old system.
- Drying a WoV plant did not create seed.

## Training and repair items

| Item | Legacy behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Repairing Armor Stand | Consumed Fragments of Power to repair worn armor when activated. |
| Training Regimen Scroll | Added one follower level; a Mighty scroll added two. The guide warned not to use a scroll for a perk-awarding level. |

For training scrolls, the old safe loop was to stop at levels 9, 19, 29, and similar thresholds,
then earn the next level through combat so the perk was awarded normally. Current Enhanced follower
progression must be checked before using that historical workflow.

## Storage and utility crafting

The Legacy specialist-storage list covered wood, metal, plant fiber, seed, brick, ore, potion,
alcohol, powder, hide, bone, liquid, modification, arrow, food, lore, key, dye, gem, bounty, plant,
and coin storage. A chest accepted only its assigned category, and modded items sometimes required a
later whitelist update. The current Enhanced [Dump Chest guide](./wov-storage-resources.md) contains
the confirmed twenty sorting destinations.

| Utility | Legacy guide details |
| --- | --- |
| Torch Bracelets | White, Green, Blue, Pink, Orange, and Red lights. A bracelet could be activated from the hotbar or worn in an accessory slot for passive light. |
| Display Racks | Four glass displays were added to the base display knowledge; the tall rack accepted spears in the old build. |
| Magic Wings | Blue, Green, Purple, and Red cosmetic wings crafted at the Elder bench with Twin Maria. They occupied an accessory slot, granted no statistics or flight, and could be equipped on a follower. |
