# Crafting & Resources

Treat crafting as a sequence: unlock the recipe, identify its station, supply its materials, and inspect the finished result.

## Follow the recipe

1. Find the knowledge or profession unlock for the intended item.
2. Check its displayed level and profession requirements.
3. Read the recipe's required station and ingredients.
4. Gather materials or use a supported material-pull action.
5. Inspect the finished item for quality, affixes, and effects before comparing it with another piece.

## Organize before expanding

Configure the storage categories you actually use. Keep supplies needed for immediate play separate from a broad deposit action, and review retained amounts before transferring mixed inventory.

## Legacy crafting catalog

The [archived WoV crafting catalog](./wov-crafting-reference.md) preserves older alchemy, food, storage, bracelet, wing, and ring information. Its recipes and values are historical references; use current Enhanced recipes for present-day requirements.
