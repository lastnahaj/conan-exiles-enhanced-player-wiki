# WoV Bosses & Bounties — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
These encounters, locations, drops, and mechanics come from the older VAM guide. They are not
current Enhanced spawn proof. Administrator replacement systems and internal logic from the source
are excluded.
:::

## Shared boss behavior in the old guide

- Every WoV boss dropped its head or carcass as a trophy for the old Bounty Board.
- A kill could also produce an Ancient Scroll Storage Box, Seed Pouch, Blueprint Treasure Box,
  Mythical Essence Bag, or one of three Valkyrian Armor Crates.
- Rarer rewards included a boss figurine for the Summoning Pool and a consumable heart that yielded
  additional resources.
- WoV bosses shared spawns with their corresponding base-game creature.
- Most bosses spawned two unharvestable minions.
- A WoV pick or pickaxe was required to harvest the matching essence, ore, and other WoV materials.

## Summoning Pool

The guide placed the WoV Summoning Pool at the end of Gallaman's Tomb in H4. Put a boss figurine on
its pillar to summon the related boss. Any of the three old boss sizes could appear.

Summoned bosses did not drop heads or boxes, but could still be harvested for WoV materials. The
guide said the Isle of Siptah pool could also work but sometimes failed to produce the boss.

## Old Bounty Board workflow

The Legacy Bounty Hunter knowledge came from the Headhunter profession. The board accepted eligible
base-game, AoC, EEWA, and VAM boss heads, awarding Bounty Tokens and separate Bounty experience.
Tokens bought rewards from its Shop tab, while individual rewards could require a Bounty rank.

The author later marked that board as crash-prone and recommended it be disabled. That old board is
not presented as a current player system. Enhanced documents the Bounty tab in the WoV hub; use the
[current hub guide](../progression/wov-professions-hub.md#bounties) for live tracking.
