# Simple Minimap

**Source mod:** Simple Minimap (by Xevyr) v5.1.9 (`3719513784`)

![Official Workshop image for Simple Minimap](/media/mods/simple-minimap.png)

Simple Minimap adds a movable, configurable minimap with points of interest, navigation markers,
clan tracking, corpse and home markers, and a separate custom-marker system.

## Minimap controls

- Hold **F1** to use the mouse with the minimap, its zoom controls, settings, and marker labels.
- Drag the player icon in the center to move the minimap.
- Choose from five saved zoom levels and adjust the overall interface scale.
- Use the top-right control to hide the minimap when it is not wanted.
- Choose between a fixed-north view and compass mode, where the map rotates around the player.

## Information it can display

- Nearby points of interest, including edge-pinned landmarks just outside the visible map area
- Clan members, unless the player or server disables tracking
- Corpse location at any distance
- Bed and bedroll markers
- Server, client, or game time through the optional clock
- Custom border color and minimap opacity, including shared hex-color presets

## Custom markers

1. Open the full map.
2. **Shift-click** the desired location.
3. Choose a marker color and label.
4. Choose local storage for cross-server use or database storage for the current game environment.
5. Optionally make the marker visible at unlimited minimap range or share it with online clan
   members.

Marker availability does not establish that a location is safe, public, or reachable on this
full-PvP server.
