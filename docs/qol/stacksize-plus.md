# Stacksize Plus

Stacksize Plus increases supported item-stack limits across base-game, DLC, and modded items.
Items that are normally non-stackable remain non-stackable.

## Refresh a changed stack

1. Move the affected stack to another inventory or container.
2. Inspect the stack again after the transfer.
3. Use the resulting in-game behavior when organizing your storage.

The mod documents transfers as the point at which changed stack settings take effect.

## Understand the multiplier

The mod supports a configurable multiplier from **1 to 100**. This describes its supported range,
not a confirmed server value. The server's **5x rates** label does not establish a stack multiplier.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does every item become stackable? | No. Normally non-stackable items remain non-stackable. |
| Why did the displayed stack behavior not change immediately? | Transfer the stack between inventories or containers to refresh it. |
| Can the limit be inferred from gathering rates? | No. These are separate settings. |
