# Mystical Travel Shelf

The Mystical Travel Shelf provides a compact alternative to the standard maproom.
Its smaller footprint changes how destinations are selected.

## Progression and recipe

The recipe is associated with the **Cartographer** feat. Check that knowledge progression and
the current crafting recipe before planning the shelf into a base.

The shelf is not documented as a free replacement for normal maproom progression.

## Destination selection

A full-size maproom allows players to move around its destination layout. The shelf uses an
**aiming-based destination system** instead. Use the shelf's displayed interaction to select a
destination; walking around it is not the intended selection method.

## Plan around the difference

| Standard maproom | Mystical Travel Shelf |
| --- | --- |
| Full-size destination layout | Compact placeable |
| Selection through movement around the layout | Aiming-based destination selection |
| Normal maproom progression | Recipe associated with Cartographer progression |

For other travel systems, see [Travel & Exploration](./index.md).
