# WoV World Areas & Meteors — Legacy Reference

::: warning Legacy-era reference
The source marked its entire map-additions section as out of date. This page preserves only the
ordinary-player material. Administrator hubs, commands, testing areas, private spaces, hidden keys,
and development tools are excluded.
:::

## Portal Room

The old Portal Room was a server-configured travel hub. Players used the portals placed there, but
the available destinations depended entirely on the server. The current Enhanced release still
documents a Portal Room; use only destinations that exist in the live room.

The older map guide expected two special portals near the Dregs entrance and another market-side
portal leading to the Portal Room. Those routes are not assumed to exist in Enhanced.

## Player Market and mine

The Legacy Player Market could hold vendors or quest NPCs and contained the entrance to the
Mythical Mine. Mine access used server-provided permits:

| Legacy access item | Recorded behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Mining Permit — old | Entered through the market gate and was consumed; the old timer reference was five minutes. |
| Mythical Mining Permit | Teleported the player into the mine and existed in five-, ten-, or fifteen-minute versions before removing the player. The guide warned that the timer could be unreliable. |
| Rusty Mine Key | Opened the deeper mine layer in addition to the normal permit requirement. |

The upper mine layer contained crystal, copper, and tin nodes. The deeper layer contained the other
WoV nodes. Permits and the key were not ordinary world drops in the old build, so their availability
depended on the server providing a public acquisition route.

Current Enhanced confirms the Player Market and Resource Mines, but not those old access items,
timers, layers, or node placement. See the [current storage and resources guide](../crafting/wov-storage-resources.md).

## VAM meteors

The old player-facing meteor loop was:

1. Watch for purple smoke and a purple meteor rain. The guide recorded a natural event in the
   Unnamed City every hour, or in the island center on Siptah.
2. Bring explosives to break the meteor shell as with Star Metal.
3. Harvest with a WoV pick for the full WoV material pool.

An ordinary tool yielded only raw gems, copper, tin, and a chance at special Iris or Sarpendon
Rocknoses in the old system. Meteor timing, locations, contents, and availability are not confirmed
for Enhanced.
