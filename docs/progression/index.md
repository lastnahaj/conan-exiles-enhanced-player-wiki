## From profession to equipment

1. Inspect your chosen recipe in the Profession tab.
2. Meet its displayed character and profession requirements, then learn the knowledge.
3. Inspect its crafting station and material requirements in the current recipe interface.
4. Use the [equipment guide](../combat/wov-arsenals-armor.md) to understand weapon families and crafting quality.
5. Check [storage and resources](../crafting/wov-storage-resources.md) for gathering tools and material sorting.

The [older bench directory](./wov-professions-benches-reference.md) is a historical reference.
Use the current recipe interface to establish which station and worker apply today.
