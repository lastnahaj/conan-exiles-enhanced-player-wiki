# Wings Of Valhalla

**Source mods:** Wings Of Valhalla (Beta) (`3719965485`) and Wings Of Valhalla Equipment (Beta)
(`3721036709`)

![Official Workshop image for Wings Of Valhalla](/media/mods/wings-of-valhalla.png)

![Official Workshop image for Wings Of Valhalla Equipment](/media/mods/wings-of-valhalla-equipment.png)

Wings Of Valhalla is a multi-system overhaul covering progression, activities, equipment,
companions, storage, and custom world areas. The guide below incorporates the current Enhanced
Workshop documentation. Because many features are configurable, a documented capability is not a
promise that it is enabled on this server.

## Player hub and progression

The documented hub brings five player areas together:

| Hub tab | Documented purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Profession | Tracks profession categories and gameplay-earned profession experience. |
| Rewards | Shows milestone rewards tied to character progression. |
| Bounty | Tracks targets drawn from base-game and Wings Of Valhalla content. |
| Currency | Presents the mod's player currency interface. |
| Settings | Holds ordinary-player choices for the system. |

The mod supports a configurable character level curve, milestone rewards, five profession
categories tied to the core stats, bounties, and extended follower leveling. The effective level
cap, experience curve, reward ladder, and follower limits are withheld because they must be checked
with this server's combined progression stack.

## Weapon arsenals and armor

The current live-build description identifies fifteen weapon arsenals:

- Ancient Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel, and Silver
- Nordic, Bone, and Chitin Scale
- Lysanthir, Unholy Ice, Pure Jade, Lava Stone, and Urghat
- Holy Serpent and Vanir Steel

Each arsenal is documented as a coordinated item family. The current build also describes random
affixes and quality tiers on crafted gear and dual wielding for base-game swords. Treat both as
server-verification items until observed here.

Documented armor families include seven Valkyrian variants—Silver, Gold, Fallen, Desert, Jungle,
Mountain, and Lava—plus Lava Legion, Mythical Crystal, and Infused Crystal armor. The companion
equipment mod specifically supplies custom weapons and Mythical Crystal armor. Acquisition routes,
recipes, stats, and PvP restrictions still require live confirmation.

## Breeding and companions

The source describes two breeding loops:

- **Rocknoses:** the documented system uses Pebblestone with metal or stone feed options and includes
  multiple Rocknose results.
- **Dragons:** eggs are heated, then hatchlings are fed meat or fish in the documented system.

Do not invest rare resources solely from this description until the relevant stations, recipes, and
results are visible on the server. Some additional creatures in the mod are not intended for normal
player acquisition and are deliberately absent here.

## Storage workflow

The documented **Dump Chest** routes deposited loot into specialized sorting chests. The current
source lists twenty destinations: Arrows, Bones, Bounties, Bricks, Currency, Dust, Dye, Food, Gems,
Keys, Leather, Liquids, Lore, Ingots, Ore, Pets, Plants, Potions, Seeds, and Wood.

These chests are described under the Wings Of Valhalla category in the construction hammer's
Decoration tab. Their recipes and availability on this server still need confirmation.

## Tools, resources, and public areas

The live-build description includes seven tool sets, fifteen new resource types, a player market, a
portal room, and resource mines. Exact nodes, routes, merchants, costs, and travel destinations are
not published until players can verify them here.

## Verification boundary

The public roadmap tracks planned, in-progress, deferred, and completed development. It is not a
release calendar and is never used as proof that a planned system is live. The final server
progression table also remains withheld until Wings Of Valhalla and EAA are tested together.
